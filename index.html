Inside Ghost Beat 3

A precision instrument, not another metronome skin.

01 — The player

Dial it in with the jog wheel.

20–300 BPM on a proper rotary wheel. Set ON bars and OFF bars, pick your time signature and subdivision, and hit play. The pads track every beat in real time.

20–300 BPM · 8 time signatures
02 — Ghost bars

Then the click walks out.

The pads turn red, the room goes quiet, and the tempo is yours to lose. Here: 90 BPM, two audible bars, one silent. Work up to one audible bar in eight, or let Random Mute drop single beats unpredictably.

SILENT · 2 on / 1 off
03 — Tap accuracy

Your timing, in milliseconds.

Tap along and get graded S through D on every session. Your first 8 taps set a timing offset, then every tap after that is measured in milliseconds.

avg deviation · consistency · grade
04 — Routines

Practice that runs itself.

Build warm-ups and workouts with timed or bar-based steps. Give each step its own tempo, ghost pattern, time signature, and accents. Loop the routine and press start once.

auto-advancing steps
05 — Progress

Proof you're getting better.

See your daily goal, weekly practice and streak in one place. Pick up your last session or take on today’s challenge.

Daily goals · weekly practice · streaks
06 — Tempo ladders

Build speed, step by step.

Pick a start and target tempo, how much to change, and how many bars to play between changes. Save it in a preset and let the player handle the tempo.

Included free · player + presets
07 — Click + counting voice

Your click. Your sound.

Play a click, spoken counts, or both. Mix their volumes separately and choose a Natural, Low, or Bright voice. Bar-based count-ins give you time to get ready.

Separate click + voice controls
08 — Routine builder

Every step, your way.

Build one step at a time. Set its length, tempo, note value, and accents, then move to the next. Keep the flow going with routine-specific sound and count-in settings.

Timed or bar-based steps · custom accents

The full stack

Everything serves one goal: time you can trust.

01

Train

Take the click away, a little at a time.

Ghost Bars

Choose how many bars play and how many go silent: 2 on, 2 off. 1 on, 3 off. The silence is where your real timing shows up.

Random Mute

Beats vanish unpredictably, so you never know which one is next. Train for real performances, where timing isn't spoon-fed.

Progressive Fade PRO

Work up to playing in total silence. Beats fade out over bars until the safety net is completely gone.

Tempo Ladders

Build toward a target tempo automatically, with a change every few bars. Available free on the player and in presets.

02

Measure

Progress in timing terms, not just minutes.

Tap Accuracy

Find out how consistent your timing really is. Your first taps set a timing offset, then every tap is measured in milliseconds and graded S through D.

Practice Stats

Streaks, session history, timing grades, and weekly charts show progress in timing terms, not just minutes.

Daily Goals

Fill your practice ring, see weekly totals, and get an optional notification when you reach your daily goal.

Daily Challenges

A new timing challenge every day. Survive ghost bars, hit accuracy targets, or hold tempo for a set time.

03

Practice

Sessions that run themselves, and count themselves.

Practice Routines

Chain exercises with BPM, ghost pattern, and time signature per step. Press play once.

Start Hands-Free

Press and hold Start to listen, then play to begin. Calibrate sensitivity for your kit and room, with optional stopping after silence.

Count Your Practice

Track playing with the app open and active, even without the click. Use your microphone, a connected audio input, or a MIDI kit. Audio stays on your device.

Counting Voices

Hear the count alongside your click. Choose Natural, Low, or Bright, with separate volumes for the click and voice.

04

Make it yours

Your sound, your setup, your library.

Deep Customization

Pick your time signature, note value, accents, click sound, bar-based count-in, and haptics. Pro adds adjustable click pitch.

Practice Notes PRO

Save reminders, sticking ideas, and what to work on next right on your routines and presets.

Private iCloud Sync

Keep routines, presets, practice history, goals, and saved notes together on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Optional, using the same Apple Account.

Room to Grow PRO

Unlimited routines, steps, and presets, plus click pitch, progressive fade, extended stats, and practice notes. One purchase, no subscription.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find it? Ask below, or email support@ghostbeat.cc.

Is Ghost Beat free?

Yes. It's free to download, with ghost bars, Random Mute, tempo ladders, and daily goals included, plus 4 routines, 6 steps per routine, and 3 presets. Pro is a one-time purchase ($4.99 in the US; the App Store shows your local price) that unlocks unlimited routines, steps, and presets, click pitch, progressive fade, extended stats, and practice notes. No subscription.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Optional private iCloud sync keeps routines, presets, practice history, goals, and saved notes together across your devices using the same Apple Account.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no Ghost Beat account. Apple handles the Apple Account used for purchases and optional iCloud sync.

Does it record my playing?

No. Microphone or connected audio input is analyzed live on your device for hands-free starts, calibration, and practice tracking. It isn't saved as a recording or uploaded, and MIDI performances aren't recorded either.

What's a ghost bar?

A bar where the click goes silent. Ghost Beat alternates audible bars with silent ones, like 2 on and 1 off, so you hold the tempo yourself. Work up to one audible bar in eight, or let Random Mute drop single beats unpredictably.
